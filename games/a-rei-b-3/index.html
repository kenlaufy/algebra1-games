    How to play

    The scale is balanced. Your job: get the mystery box alone on one pan.

    1. Build a move with the buttons: +, − or ÷, a number, and 1 or x.
    2. Apply it to one pan by clicking the pan (or the Left pan / Right pan button).
    3. The scale tips! Apply the same move to the other pan to balance it again.
    4. Solve it in as few moves as you can to earn 3 stars.

    Red tiles are negative. A +1 and a −1 cancel out. In stage 3 the scale disappears and moves apply to both sides automatically.
