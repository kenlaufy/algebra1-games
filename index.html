How the games teach

Every game follows the same three design rules.

1

The math is the move

You can't win by guessing. On the scale, the only way to get x alone is to do the same thing to both sides.

2

Concrete to abstract

Levels start with pictures, add algebra tiles and live equations, and finish with symbols only.

3

Translate it back

After every win, the game writes the student's moves as algebra and checks the answer by substitution.
