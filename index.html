How the games teach

Use the picture, make a move, and explain what changed.

1

The math is the move

Your choices change the model. In Balance Workshop, use the same operation on both sides to keep the equation balanced.

2

Concrete to abstract

Start with visible models, then connect what you see to equations, functions, and symbols.

3

Translate it back

Review the worked steps and answer checks after a win. Use them to explain why your method worked.
